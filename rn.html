Description

Geometry Dash is a fast-paced platform game where movement and music go hand in hand. Guide your geometric character through spike-filled courses, moving platforms, and sudden changes in direction. One mistimed jump sends you back to the start, so learn each section and keep the beat.

Jump in, practice the tricky parts, and make another run for the finish. The browser port is ready to play from the button above.
